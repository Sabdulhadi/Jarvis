# LUXBUS

Premium overnight sleeper coach service. Luxembourg-bound. Modeled on Japan's Dream Sleeper. Distributed via hotel partnerships to international tourists.

## Status

- **Stage:** Concept + early market research
- **Geography:** Luxembourg as the demand hub, with overnight feeders from Paris, Frankfurt, Brussels, Amsterdam (to validate)
- **Funding:** None yet — concept needs validation before raise

## The bet

Luxembourg attracts a specific traveler profile (business, finance, EU institutional, premium tourism) who would pay for a comfortable overnight transit alternative to early flights or low-cost ground transport. Hotels gain a guest-acquisition channel.

## Reference

**Japan's Dream Sleeper** (Ryobi / WILLER Express) — premium private-cabin overnight buses. Lie-flat or near-lie-flat seats. Privacy partitions. Curated arrival experience.

## Current asks

1. **Demand sizing.** Overnight inbound passenger volumes Luxembourg ↔ Paris / Frankfurt / Brussels / Amsterdam. Existing competitors (Flixbus, BlaBlaCar Bus, train, low-cost air).
2. **Vehicle research.** Coach manufacturers offering sleeper / lie-flat conversions: Setra, Van Hool, Volvo, Scania — plus Japanese references for spec inspiration.
3. **Hotel-partnership pitch.** What's the value to hotels? Guest-acquisition channel? Cross-promotion? Revenue share?
4. **Regulatory check.** Cross-border overnight passenger transport in EU — driver hours regulation (Reg 561/2006), licensing for premium long-distance services.

## Subfolders

- `market-research/` — demand data, competitor scans, pricing benchmarks
- `operations/` — vehicle spec, route planning, driver staffing
- `partnerships/` — hotel pitches, tourism boards, OTAs

## Open questions

- Asset-light (lease + driver contract) or asset-heavy (own coaches)?
- Single Luxembourg hub or multi-hub from inception?
- Partnership-first launch (one anchor hotel chain) or market-launch?
