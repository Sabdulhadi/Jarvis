# Energy drink (working title TBD)

Hibiscus-based functional energy drink. Positioning loosely modeled on Celsius — clean label, functional benefit, lifestyle brand rather than medicine or extreme-sport prop.

## Status

- **Stage:** Earliest. Ideation.
- **Funding:** None. Concept needs formulation + brand work before any spend.

## Why hibiscus

- Strong color (visual differentiation on shelf)
- Cardiovascular and antioxidant story (verifiable, not hype)
- Cultural associations — North African, Caribbean, South Asian — that broaden the demographic
- Underused in the energy category vs the saturated yerba / guarana / matcha pool

## Subfolders

- `formulation/` — ingredient rationale, target spec sheet, co-packer briefs
- `branding/` — name shortlist, positioning, visual direction
- `market-research/` — Celsius / Red Bull / Prime / Yerba Mate / Bang category scans

## Current asks

1. **Formulation brief.** Caffeine source (natural — green coffee bean? guayusa? matcha?), hibiscus extract spec, sweetener strategy (zero-cal vs low-cal vs natural), target sugar / calorie profile, vitamin/electrolyte fortification.
2. **Brand positioning.** What gap does this fill? Female-skewing premium? Cultural-crossover lifestyle? Heart-health functional? Pick one and own it.
3. **Co-packer scan.** France, Belgium, Netherlands. Beverage contract manufacturers with hot-fill or aseptic capability for botanical extracts.

## Open questions

- Ready-to-drink can vs powder sachet for first launch?
- Direct-to-consumer (web + Amazon) or retail-first (specialty grocery, gym chains)?
- Hibiscus sourcing — Egypt, Sudan, Mexico — and what story sells best to the target audience?
