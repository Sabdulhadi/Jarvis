# Book project — Vitamins, reworked

Reworking my mother's book on vitamins into the accessible, vivid voice of Philipp Dettmer's *Immune*.

## The bet

There's a gap between the wellness-cliché vitamin book (which over-promises) and the textbook (which under-engages). Dettmer proved the model: rigorous science wrapped in story and metaphor, sold to readers who don't read science books. Vitamins are an ideal subject — universally relevant, biochemically rich, full of misunderstood mechanisms.

## Status

- **Stage:** Pre-import. Source manuscript not yet in repo.
- **Voice calibration:** Pending pilot chapter

## Voice reference

**Philipp Dettmer — *Immune*.** Cells as characters. Mechanisms as factories and armies. Curiosity as the engine. One concept per scene. Awe as the default register.

## Subfolders

## Hard rules

- Every load-bearing scientific claim cites a primary source (PubMed, peer-reviewed reviews, NIH/WHO/EFSA consensus).
- Mother's clinical perspective is preserved — the rework reframes, doesn't contradict. Factual errors are flagged before being changed.
- No wellness-marketing language. Strip every "boosts immunity" cliché.
