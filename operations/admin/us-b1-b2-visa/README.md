# US B1/B2 visa — Paris embassy

Family visit (grandmother, Tucson, Arizona).

## Status

- **Stage:** Pre-application
- **Embassy:** Paris (US Embassy France)
- **Expected wait:** ~4 months from appointment booking to interview
- **Sponsor:** Father (financial)

## Document checklist

- [ ] DS-160 form completed online
- [ ] DS-160 confirmation page printed
- [ ] Visa fee paid ($185 currently — verify)
- [ ] Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond intended stay
- [ ] Photo per US embassy specs (5x5 cm, white background)
- [ ] Appointment booked via US embassy France portal
- [ ] Proof of ties to France: lease, employment / business proof (Passeport Talent, INDROX docs), bank statements
- [ ] Purpose of visit documentation: grandmother's address in Tucson, evidence of family relationship
- [ ] Sponsor documents from father: notarized sponsorship letter, recent bank statements, recent tax returns, proof of relationship
- [ ] Travel itinerary (tentative — don't book before visa is approved)

## Sponsor letter — what to ask father for

- Notarized sponsorship letter stating intent to cover expenses
- Last 3-6 months of bank statements
- Most recent tax return / equivalent
- Proof of relationship (birth certificate of Syed or family registry)
- Letter should explicitly mention: duration of intended visit, that costs will be covered, that no employment in the US is intended

## Action sequence

1. Complete DS-160 ASAP
2. Book appointment ASAP — the 4-month wait is the binding constraint
3. Compile supporting documents in parallel
4. Pay visa fee
5. Attend interview prepared with tie-to-France narrative front and center

## Risks

- Pakistani-French dual context may invite extra scrutiny — mitigate by leading with strong ties to France (INDROX, residence, family if applicable)
- Don't volunteer immigration intent toward Canada or UK in the interview — irrelevant and confusing
